## 🎯 Lesson Overview & Objectives

Dataset Endorsement and Discovery in Power BI Service. Covers Certified Datasets, Promoted Datasets, tenant governance, and making data discoverable across organizational units.

**Key Skills & Concepts Covered:**
- ✅ **Endorsement Workflow (Promoted vs Certified)**
- ✅ **Enabling Discoverability for Unauthorized Users**
- ✅ **Data Stewardship & Governance Policies**
- ✅ **Self-service BI Architecture**

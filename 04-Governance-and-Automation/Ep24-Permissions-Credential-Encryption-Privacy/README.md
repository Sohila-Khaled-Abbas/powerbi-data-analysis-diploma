## 🎯 Lesson Overview & Objectives

Mastering Power BI Permissions, Credential Management, Encryption, and Data Privacy Levels (Public, Organizational, Private). Critical for preventing firewall formula errors.

**Key Skills & Concepts Covered:**
- ✅ **Privacy Levels (Private, Organizational, Public)**
- ✅ **Solving 'Formula.Firewall: Query references other queries' Error**
- ✅ **Encryption Options in Data Sources**
- ✅ **Global vs Local Permissions**

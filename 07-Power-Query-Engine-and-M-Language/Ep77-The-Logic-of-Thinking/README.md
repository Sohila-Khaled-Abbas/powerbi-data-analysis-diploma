## 🎯 Lesson Overview & Objectives

The Logic of Thinking in Power Query. Philosophical and architectural masterclass by Essam Elsayed on how expert data engineers decompose complex data problems into clean, declarative transformations.

**Key Skills & Concepts Covered:**
- ✅ **Decomposition of Complex Business Requirements**
- ✅ **Thinking in Sets and Transformations (Not Procedural Loops)**
- ✅ **Anticipating Edge Cases and Upstream Failures**
- ✅ **Senior Data Analyst Mindset**

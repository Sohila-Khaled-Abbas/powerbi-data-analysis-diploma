## 🎯 Lesson Overview & Objectives

Connecting to published Power BI Semantic Models (formerly Datasets). Explains reusable enterprise data models, single source of truth, and thin reports connecting via Live Connection.

**Key Skills & Concepts Covered:**
- ✅ **Power BI Semantic Models**
- ✅ **Thin Reports vs Thick Models**
- ✅ **Single Version of the Truth Architecture**
- ✅ **Reusing Certified Datasets**

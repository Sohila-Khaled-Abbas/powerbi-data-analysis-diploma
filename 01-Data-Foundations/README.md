## 📖 Module Overview

Establishes the fundamental architecture of business intelligence projects and teaches the principles of proper raw data structuring (Tidy Data). You will understand the crucial distinction between human-readable presentation layouts and database-ready tabular structures required by Power BI's VertiPaq tabular model.

### 🎯 Key Competencies Acquired:

- 🌟 **End-to-End Business Intelligence Project Lifecycle**
- 🌟 **Tidy Data Rules: Observations, Variables, and Units**
- 🌟 **Diagnosing Anti-Patterns (Merged cells, repeated banners, cross-matrices)**
- 🌟 **Preparing Unstructured Raw Data for High-Performance Modeling**

## 🎯 Lesson Overview & Objectives

Real-World Case Study: Complex Header Structure. Deconstructs an intricate enterprise spreadsheet with 4 levels of nested headers, merged category blocks, and blank spacer rows.

**Key Skills & Concepts Covered:**
- ✅ **Deconstructing Multi-Tier Headers**
- ✅ **Transpose, Fill Down & Concatenate Strategy**
- ✅ **Filtering Blank Intermediary Rows**
- ✅ **Automating Monthly Import Templates**

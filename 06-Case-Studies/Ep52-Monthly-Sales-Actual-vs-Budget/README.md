## 🎯 Lesson Overview & Objectives

Real-World Case Study: Monthly Sales (Actual vs Budget). Models disparate operational actuals and financial budget spreadsheets with different granularities into a clean Star Schema.

- ✅ **Reconciling Different Granularities (Daily vs Monthly)**

- ✅ **Shared Dimension Modeling**
- ✅ **Actual vs Budget Variance Analysis**
